# Service card imagery

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-09-07. These are conceptual illustrations, not photographs of company infrastructure or screenshots of delivered client work.

All six images were generated and visually reviewed before being connected to `src/components/Services.jsx`. Each PNG is kept here as the final project asset, with its full prompt below. Original shared images in `src/images/` are retained for the pages that already use them.

The artwork leaves dark space at the left for desktop copy. Desktop CSS fits the full illustration to the card height; mobile CSS shows a crop of the right side above the copy. Keep the text in HTML rather than baking it into replacement artwork.
